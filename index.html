EasyNotes

Free for everyone

  • Free & Offline. No registration, no subscription, no internet needed – your notes stay on your PC
  • Topics, sub-topics, pages. Rich pages, spreadsheets and checklists with autosave, undo across the session and a dashboard
  • Pictures, shapes and icons. Side-by-side pictures, 179 shapes that live with your paragraphs, 1,850 line icons
  • Import what you have. Word, PDF, Excel / CSV, PowerPoint, web pages and OneNote notebooks – into any topic
  • Session Clipboard and Transit. Everything you copy while the app runs; multi-select pieces of a page and carry them anywhere
  • Reminders and checklists. A reminder on any text – two clicks to set, one to get back to it – and a to-do list in the header
  • MCP supported. Your AI tools work with your notes through a local endpoint: 48 tools, scoped tokens and rules you set. Off by default
  • Verified backups. Your location, schedule and retention – every backup checked before it is kept
  • Content. Native support for a broad variety of content
